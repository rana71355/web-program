FESTIVALS OF INDIA

india is a land of diverse cultures and tradition: various festivals are celebrated.

1.DIWALI

Diwali, known as the festival of lights, is a major celebration of the spiritual victory of light over darkness and good over evil.

2.DURGA PUJA

>Durga Puja is a major annual Hindu festival that celebrates the victory of the goddess Durga over the shape-shifting demon Mahishasura, symbolizing the triumph of good over evil.

3.HOLI

Holi, widely known as the Festival of Colors, is a joyous Hindu celebration that marks the arrival of spring and the end of winter.
