Holi

"Holi, widely known as the Festival of Colors, is a joyous Hindu celebration that marks the arrival of spring and the end of winter. The festivities begin the night before the main day with Holika Dahan, a sacred bonfire ritual that symbolizes the burning of evil and commemorates the legendary devotion of Prahlad. The following day, known as Rangwali Holi or Dhulandi, brings communities together as people joyfully smear bright colored powders (gulal), splash colored water, dance to traditional music, and share festive sweets like gujiya. Ultimately, the festival stands as a powerful cultural symbol of unity, forgiveness, renewal, and the triumph of good over evil."
