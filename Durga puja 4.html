Durga Puja

"Durga Puja is one of the most prominent, vibrant, and widely celebrated Hindu festivals in India, particularly in West Bengal, where it is recognized as a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage. The festival honors Goddess Durga and celebrates her victory over the shape-shifting demon Mahishasura, symbolizing the ultimate triumph of good over evil. Traditionally occurring over five major days during the autumn month of Ashwin, it also marks the annual homecoming of the Goddess to Earth, accompanied by her children: Lakshmi, Saraswati, Ganesha, and Kartikeya."
