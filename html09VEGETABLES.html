vegetables are essential, nutrient-dense parts of plants that form the foundation of a healthy human diet. Whether harvested as roots, leaves, stems, or seeds, they provide an abundance of dietary fiber, vitamins, and minerals while remaining naturally low in calories and fat. Incorporating a colorful variety of vegetables into daily meals is widely recognized by health experts to support robust digestive health, boost immune function, and significantly lower the long-term risk of chronic illnesses such as heart disease and stroke.
