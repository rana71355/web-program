DIWALI

"Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is the Hindu Festival of Lights and one of the most widely celebrated festivals in India and around the world. The word "Deepavali" originates from Sanskrit, meaning a "row of lighted lamps."It fundamentally celebrates the spiritual victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance.Diwali, the Hindu Festival of Lights, is a vibrant five-day celebration that symbolizes the spiritual victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance. Rooted in ancient Indian mythology, it most famously marks the return of Lord Rama to Ayodhya after 14 years of exile, an event celebrated by illuminating homes with thousands of earthen oil lamps called diyas. Today, families around the world honor this joyous occasion by cleaning and decorating their homes with colorful rangoli patterns, wearing new clothes, and gathering for a special evening Lakshmi Puja to welcome prosperity and good fortune. The festival is defined by a spirit of generosity, characterized by the sharing of traditional sweets, the exchanging of gifts, and spectacular firework displays that light up the night sky."
