ABOUT US

MEA Engineering College (MEAEC) is a private, AICTE-approved institution established in 2002 in Malappuram, Kerala, and affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University. It offers NBA-accredited B.Tech, M.Tech, and MCA programs on a modern 25-acre campus known for its strong placement cell.
